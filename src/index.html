FAQ

What is the cost of a mobile application?

The cost depends on the number of screens, the complexity of the logic, integrations with external services and the platforms you need.

After a short call we prepare a detailed estimate with a breakdown by stages, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

Do you provide a guarantee for the mobile application?

Yes. We fix any errors found after the release free of charge during the warranty period of 6 months.

After that, you can continue with technical support under a separate agreement.

How long will development take?

Development terms directly depend on the requirements for the mobile application, the characteristics of the company, as well as the wishes of the customer.

Average development time from start to finished application:
Medium projects up to 3 months.
Large projects about 4-6 months.
To get a more accurate estimate of the project completion time, it is necessary to determine the main task of the application, think over its logic and functionality.

I will not tell my idea, do you guarantee confidentiality?

Of course. Before discussing the details we sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA).

All rights to the source code and design are transferred to you after the project is completed.
